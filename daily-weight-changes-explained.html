Why Your Weight Changes From Day to Day

The number on a scale can change from one day to the next even when your routine is unchanged. Short-term changes often reflect water and digestive contents rather than changes in body fat.

Common reasons for daily changes

Fluid balance, sodium intake, carbohydrate intake, food volume, bowel movements, exercise and hormonal changes can all influence scale weight.

Use trends instead of single readings

Weighing under similar conditions and reviewing a multi-day or multi-week trend can give a more useful picture than comparing two individual days.

What about sudden changes?

Large or persistent unexplained changes may have causes that deserve professional assessment, especially when accompanied by symptoms such as swelling, weakness or shortness of breath.

Stay consistent

Keep your measurement routine and focus on longer-term patterns when evaluating progress.
