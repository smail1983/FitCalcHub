BMI Calculator Guide: How BMI Works and What It Means

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a simple screening measure based on height and weight. This guide explains how BMI is calculated and how to interpret the result.

What is BMI?

BMI is calculated by dividing body weight in kilograms by height in meters squared. For adults, the result is commonly grouped into screening categories, but BMI does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

How to calculate BMI

The formula is: BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m). If using pounds and inches, the calculation uses the corresponding conversion factor.

Common adult BMI categories

For adults, a BMI below 18.5 is commonly classified as underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 as a healthy-weight range, 25.0 to 29.9 as overweight, and 30.0 or higher as obesity. These ranges are screening guidelines, not a diagnosis.

Why BMI has limitations

BMI cannot distinguish between muscle, bone and fat mass. A muscular person may have a higher BMI without having excess body fat. Age, body composition, health history and other factors also matter when assessing health.

Use BMI as one piece of information

Use BMI together with other relevant information rather than relying on one number. Focus on sustainable nutrition, regular physical activity and long-term health habits. If you have specific medical or nutritional concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
