How to use the meal calorie and macro calculator

Add each food in your meal as a separate row. Enter its serving quantity and the nutrition values for one serving: calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat. The calculator multiplies each value by the quantity and adds the rows to show your meal's total calories and macronutrients.

What calories and macros mean

Calories describe the energy supplied by food. Protein supports the maintenance and repair of body tissues. Carbohydrates are an important energy source, and fat supports several normal body functions and helps the body absorb certain vitamins. The balance of these nutrients varies with food choices and individual needs.

Tips for more useful nutrition totals

  • Use nutrition information from the food label or a reliable food database.
  • Make sure the serving size in the nutrition information matches the quantity you enter.
  • Enter values for the same preparation state, such as cooked rice or dry rice, rather than mixing them.
  • Include oils, sauces, dressings and toppings when they are part of the meal.
  • Use consistent units and avoid treating estimated values as exact laboratory measurements.

Understanding the result

The totals are only as accurate as the food and serving information you enter. Brand, recipe, cooking method, water loss and portion size can change nutrition values. This tool does not automatically identify foods or retrieve nutrition data; you supply the values for each row.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator recognize food automatically?

No. Enter the calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat shown for each food serving.

Can I calculate a whole meal with several foods?

Yes. Select Add Food to create another row, then enter the serving quantity and nutrition values for each ingredient or food item.

Are the calorie and macro totals exact?

They are arithmetic totals based on your entries. Nutrition labels and food databases are estimates and may vary by brand, recipe and preparation.
