HEALTH & BODY COMPOSITION

BMI by Age: What the Number Means

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a screening measure based on height and weight. For adults, the standard BMI categories are generally applied without separate adult cutoffs for each age.

What BMI can tell you

BMI can provide a quick way to describe weight relative to height, but it does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass or where fat is distributed.

Why age still matters

Age can be relevant when interpreting overall health, fitness and body composition. A BMI number should therefore be considered alongside other information rather than used by itself.

When BMI is not enough

Athletes with substantial muscle mass and people with different body-composition profiles can have BMI values that do not fully describe their health. For personal medical interpretation, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
