How to Build a Simple Walking Routine

Walking is an accessible way to add regular physical activity to your week. Start comfortably and increase your activity gradually.

Start with a realistic target

Choose a duration and pace you can repeat consistently. For many beginners, several short walks can be easier to maintain than one long session.

Make walking more useful

  • Walk at a comfortable moderate pace.
  • Increase time or distance gradually.
  • Use hills or short pace changes when appropriate.
  • Choose comfortable footwear and safe routes.
  • Track your walks to see your consistency.

Consistency matters

Regular physical activity can support cardiovascular health, mood and overall fitness. If you have been inactive or have a health condition, choose an appropriate starting level and seek professional advice when needed.
