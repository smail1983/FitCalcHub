Choose the right health and fitness calculator

FitCalcHub brings together practical online fitness calculators for everyday health, nutrition and activity planning. Each tool provides an estimate based on the information you enter. Results can help you understand common measurements, but they are not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for personalized professional advice.

Calorie and energy calculators

Use the TDEE calculator to estimate total daily energy expenditure and the BMR calculator to estimate basal metabolic rate. The calorie deficit calculator offers an illustrative comparison with estimated maintenance calories.

Body measurement calculators

The BMI calculator estimates body mass index from height and weight. The body fat percentage calculator uses the US Navy circumference method to estimate body fat.

Meal nutrition calculator

With the meal calorie and macro calculator, you can add foods and total the calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat using nutrition values that you provide.

How to use calculator results

Enter accurate measurements, check the units and review the explanation on each calculator page. Estimates may vary because of individual differences, measurement technique and the assumptions used by each equation. For advice about a medical condition, nutrition plan or weight-management goal, speak with a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian.
