How to Calculate a Calorie Deficit

A calorie deficit occurs when energy intake is lower than energy expenditure over time. A simple starting point is to estimate TDEE and then choose a moderate reduction.

Step 1: Estimate TDEE

Your TDEE is an estimate of the calories you use each day based on resting needs and activity.

Step 2: Choose a moderate reduction

A common planning approach is to reduce daily intake by a few hundred calories rather than making an extreme change. Your actual needs can vary.

Step 3: Track and adjust

Use trends over several weeks rather than a single day's weight. If you have a medical condition or special dietary needs, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
