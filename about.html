About FitCalcHub

FitCalcHub is a collection of free health and fitness calculators and plain-language articles. Our goal is simple: to help you understand numbers such as calorie needs, BMI and body fat so you can make more informed everyday decisions.

What We Offer

  • Calculators: TDEE and daily calorie estimates, BMI, body fat percentage, and meal calories and macros. No account is needed.
  • Articles: Practical guides about calories, protein, BMR vs TDEE and training basics.

How Our Calculators Work

Each calculator explains the formula it uses. For example, our TDEE calculator estimates basal metabolic rate using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and multiplies that estimate by an activity factor. The results are estimates, not exact measurements.

Who Is Behind FitCalcHub?

FitCalcHub is created and maintained by Ismail Abamran. The site was built to make common health and fitness calculations easier to access and understand through straightforward tools and educational content.

How We Approach Accuracy

  • Calculator formulas are based on published, widely used equations.
  • Articles are intended to explain health and fitness topics in clear, accessible language.
  • We welcome corrections and feedback. If you find an error or have a suggestion, please contact us through our Contact page.

Important Notice

FitCalcHub provides general educational information only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
