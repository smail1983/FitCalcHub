Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

FitCalcHub provides free health and fitness calculators and educational content. We respect your privacy and explain below how information may be handled when you use our website.

Information We Collect

Our calculators can be used without an account. Values entered into calculators are used to generate results in your browser and are not intended to identify you personally. We may also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited and general website usage information.

Cookies

FitCalcHub uses a first-party consent cookie to remember your optional advertising choice across pages and visits in the same browser. You can change your choice using the “Privacy settings” link in the footer.

Advertising and Consent

FitCalcHub displays an advertising consent banner to all visitors. Optional advertising is off unless you choose “Accept advertising”. If you choose “Reject optional advertising”, that choice is saved in the consent cookie and optional advertising remains disabled on later pages and visits using the same browser. The website loads the Monetag advertising script only after you accept the advertising category. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time by selecting “Privacy settings” in the footer. Monetag and its advertising partners may process technical information and use cookies or similar technologies when the advertising script is enabled. See the Monetag Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

Analytics

Analytics services may be used to understand website traffic and improve our calculators, content and user experience.

Third-Party Links

FitCalcHub may link to external websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review their policies separately.

Children's Privacy

FitCalcHub is not specifically directed toward children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with our website, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the contact information provided on FitCalcHub.
