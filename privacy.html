Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

FitCalcHub provides free health and fitness calculators and educational content. We respect your privacy and explain below how information may be handled when you use our website.

Information We Collect

Our calculators can be used without an account. Values entered into calculators are used to generate results in your browser and are not intended to identify you personally. We may also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited and general website usage information.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The calculator JavaScript reviewed in this website code does not call document.cookie or the browser storage APIs (localStorage or sessionStorage). This does not mean that no cookies or similar technologies are used on the website: the third-party Monetag advertising script is loaded on pages where it is included and may use its own cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings, although browser controls may not cover every type of device storage or identifier.

Advertising

FitCalcHub may display advertisements provided by Monetag. Monetag and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process technical information, such as information about your browser or device, IP address, approximate location, pages visited and interactions with advertisements, to deliver and measure ads, manage ad frequency and help prevent invalid activity. The exact information and technologies used may depend on the ad format, your device, settings and location.

Third-party advertising providers determine their own data practices. FitCalcHub does not control how Monetag or its partners collect and use information through their advertising technologies. Please review their policies for details:

  • Monetag Privacy Policy
  • Monetag Cookies Policy
  • Monetag Advertising Opt-Out

Your Advertising and Cookie Choices

You can block or delete cookies using your browser settings. You can also review available advertising opt-out options through AboutAds in the United States or Your Online Choices in Europe. These industry opt-out tools may not cover every advertising provider or every device.

Where applicable law requires consent before non-essential advertising technologies are used, we must obtain the required consent before loading or using those technologies. Please note that this policy statement does not itself provide a consent banner or prevent the advertising script from loading.

Calculator Inputs and Sharing

The calculator scripts reviewed for BMI, Body Fat, BMR, TDEE, Calorie Deficit and Meal Calories perform their calculations in the browser. In those calculator scripts, we found no calls to fetch, XMLHttpRequest or sendBeacon to transmit calculator entries, and no use of localStorage or sessionStorage to save those entries. The Meal Calories calculator keeps the food rows in a JavaScript variable while the page is open; the reviewed code does not save them for a later visit. The calculator code also does not directly write cookies. These statements describe the calculator code we reviewed and do not cover data that may be processed independently by third-party scripts, hosting services or the browser.

The TDEE calculator has a user-triggered Copy Results button and Share Results button. Copy Results copies the displayed TDEE result to the clipboard. Share Results opens the device or browser share sheet with the result text and current page URL; if native sharing is unavailable, it copies the result text and URL to the clipboard. FitCalcHub does not receive the result through this feature. If you choose an external app or service in the share sheet, that service handles the information you choose to share under its own terms and privacy policy.

Third-Party Links

FitCalcHub may link to external websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review their policies separately.

Children's Privacy

FitCalcHub is not specifically directed toward children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with our website, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use our Contact page.
