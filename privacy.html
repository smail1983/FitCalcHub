Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

FitCalcHub provides free health and fitness calculators and educational content. We respect your privacy and explain below how information may be handled when you use our website.

Information We Collect

Our calculators can be used without an account. Values entered into calculators are used to generate results in your browser and are not intended to identify you personally. We may also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited and general website usage information.

Cookies and Consent Preference Cookie

FitCalcHub uses a first-party cookie named fitcalchub_consent to store your consent categories and remember whether you accepted or rejected optional advertising. The cookie is used to apply your choice across pages and visits in the same browser; it is configured to expire after 180 days. The strictly necessary category stores your preference. You can change your choice at any time using the “Privacy settings” link in the footer. Clearing browser cookies will also clear the saved choice.

Advertising and Consent

FitCalcHub displays an advertising consent banner to all visitors, without region-based detection. Optional advertising is off unless you choose “Accept advertising”. Choosing “Reject optional advertising” saves that choice in the consent preference cookie. The Monetag advertising script is loaded only after the advertising category is accepted, and it is not loaded on the Privacy, Terms, Medical Disclaimer or Contact pages. If you change an existing acceptance to rejection through “Privacy settings” on a page where Monetag has already loaded, the page reloads so that the advertising script is no longer active in the new document. The saved rejection prevents it from loading again on later pages and visits in that browser.

When loaded on eligible pages after consent, Monetag and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process technical information, such as browser or device information, IP address, approximate location, pages visited and interactions with advertisements, to deliver and measure ads, manage ad frequency and help prevent invalid activity. The exact information and technologies used may depend on the ad format, device, settings and location. FitCalcHub does not control the independent data practices of those providers. Please review their policies:

  • Monetag Privacy Policy
  • Monetag Cookies Policy
  • Monetag Advertising Opt-Out

You can also review industry opt-out options through AboutAds in the United States or Your Online Choices in Europe. These tools may not cover every provider or device.

Calculator Inputs and Sharing

The calculator scripts reviewed for BMI, Body Fat, BMR, TDEE, Calorie Deficit and Meal Calories perform their calculations in the browser. In those calculator scripts, no calls to fetch, XMLHttpRequest or sendBeacon were found to transmit calculator entries, and no use of localStorage or sessionStorage was found to save those entries. The Meal Calories calculator keeps food rows in a JavaScript variable while the page is open; the reviewed code does not save them for a later visit. The calculator code also does not directly write cookies. These statements describe the calculator code reviewed and do not cover data that may be processed independently by third-party scripts, hosting services or the browser.

The TDEE calculator has a user-triggered Copy Results button and Share Results button. Copy Results copies the displayed TDEE result to the clipboard. Share Results opens the device or browser share sheet with the result text and current page URL; if native sharing is unavailable, it copies the result text and URL to the clipboard. FitCalcHub does not receive the result through this feature. If you choose an external app or service in the share sheet, that service handles the information you choose to share under its own terms and privacy policy.

Third-Party Links

FitCalcHub may link to external websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please review their policies separately.

Children's Privacy

FitCalcHub is not specifically directed toward children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with our website, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the contact information provided on FitCalcHub.
