Fiber: What It Is and How to Get More

Learn what dietary fiber does and discover simple ways to include more fiber-rich foods in everyday meals.

Where fiber comes from

Fiber is found naturally in plant foods such as vegetables, fruits, beans, lentils, whole grains, nuts and seeds.

Easy ways to add more

  • Add berries or an apple to breakfast.
  • Choose oats or whole grains more often.
  • Add beans or lentils to soups and salads.
  • Keep vegetables visible and ready to eat.
  • Add nuts or seeds to yogurt and meals.

Increase gradually

If your current intake is low, increase fiber gradually and drink enough fluids. Individual needs vary, so focus on a balanced overall eating pattern.

Health information: This article is for general education and does not replace individualized medical or nutrition advice.
