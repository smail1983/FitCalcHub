Calorie Deficit Guide

Updated September 2026

What is a calorie deficit?

A calorie deficit means energy intake is below energy expenditure over time. Maintenance calories are an estimate of the energy needed to maintain body weight under a particular routine.

Why calculators are only estimates

Energy needs differ between people and can change over time. A calculator can provide a useful starting point, but it cannot observe your actual daily energy expenditure.

Choose a practical approach

Moderate changes are often easier to maintain than extreme restrictions. Your calorie needs, nutrition, activity, medications and health history all matter. Avoid treating a generic calculator result as individualized medical advice.

When to get professional guidance

Talk with a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have a medical condition, take medications affected by diet or weight, or have concerns about disordered eating.
