How the US Navy body fat calculator works

This free body fat percentage calculator uses the US Navy circumference method to produce an estimate from your height and selected body measurements. For men, the calculation uses waist, neck and height. For women, it also uses hip circumference. Enter all measurements in centimetres, and measure consistently without pulling the tape tightly into the skin.

How to take accurate measurements

  • Height: Stand upright without shoes and measure from the floor to the top of your head.
  • Waist: Measure around the abdomen at the method's recommended waist level, keeping the tape horizontal.
  • Neck: Measure around the neck with the tape level and relaxed.
  • Hips: For the female equation, measure around the widest part of the hips.

Use the same measuring locations and similar conditions whenever you repeat the estimate. Small differences in tape position or posture can change the result.

Body fat percentage vs. BMI

BMI compares weight with height and does not directly measure body fat. A body fat estimate attempts to describe the proportion of body weight made up of fat tissue. Both are screening tools and neither provides a complete picture of health, fitness or body composition.

Limitations of this body fat estimate

The US Navy method is an equation-based estimate, not a direct measurement. It may differ from results produced by skinfold testing, bioelectrical impedance scales or clinical body-composition methods. Do not use one result to diagnose a health condition or make extreme changes to diet or exercise. For medical or individualized advice, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Frequently asked questions

Is the body fat calculator accurate?

It provides an estimate based on circumference measurements. Measuring technique and individual body shape can affect the result, so it should not be treated as an exact measurement.

Why does the calculator ask for hip measurements?

The female version of the US Navy equation uses waist, hip and neck measurements, while the male version uses waist and neck measurements.

How often should I check body fat?

If you choose to track it, use the same method and measurement conditions and focus on longer-term trends rather than day-to-day changes.
