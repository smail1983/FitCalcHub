Macro Calculator Guide: How to Calculate Protein, Carbs and Fat

Macronutrients are protein, carbohydrates and fat. They provide energy and play different roles in a balanced diet.

Why estimates vary

Individual results can differ because body composition, activity, diet, environment and other factors vary from person to person. Calculator results should therefore be treated as estimates.

Use the result as a starting point

Combine calculator estimates with consistent habits and longer-term trends. Avoid extreme diet or exercise changes. If you have a medical condition or special nutritional needs, consider advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
