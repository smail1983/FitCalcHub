Healthy Meal Prep: A Beginner's 5-Step Guide

Use a simple meal-prep routine to save time and make balanced choices easier during busy days.

1. Pick a few meals

Choose two or three breakfasts, lunches or dinners that you enjoy and can prepare easily.

2. Build balanced plates

Combine a protein source, vegetables or fruit, a carbohydrate source and an appropriate amount of healthy fat.

3. Shop with a list

Plan ingredients before shopping. This can reduce food waste and make your week easier.

4. Prepare flexible ingredients

Cook a batch of grains, protein and vegetables that can be mixed into different meals rather than preparing identical containers.

5. Keep it sustainable

Meal prep should make life easier, not create unnecessary stress. Adjust portions and foods to your preferences, budget and goals.
