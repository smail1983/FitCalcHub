What to Eat After a Workout: A Simple Guide

A practical look at protein, carbohydrates, fluids and balanced meals after exercise.

Think protein + carbohydrates + fluids

After exercise, a balanced meal can provide protein for tissue repair, carbohydrates to replenish energy stores and fluids to replace losses.

Simple meal combinations

  • Chicken, rice and vegetables.
  • Eggs, potatoes and fruit.
  • Greek yogurt, oats and berries.
  • Beans, whole grains and vegetables.
  • Fish, potatoes and a large salad.

Do you need a special recovery product?

Not necessarily. Many people can meet their needs with ordinary foods and drinks. The best choice depends on training demands, total diet and personal preferences.
