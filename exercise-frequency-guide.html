How Often Should You Exercise to Stay Healthy?

A sustainable exercise routine combines regular movement with enough recovery. The best schedule is one you can follow consistently and adapt to your fitness level.

Combine different types of activity

A balanced routine can include aerobic activity, strength training and everyday movement. Variety can support overall fitness while reducing reliance on one type of exercise.

Start gradually

If you have been inactive, begin with manageable sessions and increase duration or intensity gradually. Consistency is more useful than starting with an overly demanding plan.

Recovery matters

Rest and recovery allow you to adapt to training. Sleep, nutrition and easier days can all be part of a sustainable routine.

Listen to your body

Persistent pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath or other concerning symptoms should not be ignored. Seek appropriate professional advice when needed.
