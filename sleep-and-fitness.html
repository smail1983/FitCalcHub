Why Sleep Matters for Fitness and Healthy Weight

Sleep is part of a healthy lifestyle and can support recovery, daily energy and long-term health.

Recovery is more than exercise

Training creates a physical demand on the body. Recovery includes adequate food, hydration, rest and sleep. Treating sleep as part of your routine can make a fitness plan more sustainable.

Simple sleep habits

  • Keep a consistent sleep and wake schedule.
  • Create a relaxing wind-down routine.
  • Keep the bedroom comfortable, quiet and dark.
  • Limit stimulating activities close to bedtime.
  • Prioritize sleep instead of adding unnecessary training volume.

When to seek help

Persistent sleep problems can have many causes. If poor sleep continues or affects daytime functioning, consider discussing it with a healthcare professional.
