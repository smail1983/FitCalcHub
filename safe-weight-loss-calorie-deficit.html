How to Lose Weight Safely With a Calorie Deficit

Weight management usually involves balancing food intake, physical activity, sleep, and other factors. A moderate calorie deficit can be part of a weight-loss plan.

Focus on sustainable habits

Prioritize nutrient-dense foods, adequate protein and fiber, regular movement, and realistic goals. Avoid extreme restriction.

Monitor trends

Daily body weight can fluctuate. Looking at longer-term trends gives a more useful picture of change.

People who are pregnant, under 18, have a history of eating disorders, or have medical conditions should seek professional guidance before changing calorie intake.
