How to Calculate Your Daily Calorie Needs

Daily calorie needs can be estimated by calculating BMR and then accounting for activity. This produces an estimate of TDEE.

1. Estimate BMR

BMR uses factors such as age, sex, height, and weight.

2. Account for activity

Activity multipliers provide an estimate of total daily energy expenditure.

3. Use the result as a starting point

Your real needs may differ from a calculator estimate. Track your progress and adjust thoughtfully.
