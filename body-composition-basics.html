Body Composition Basics: BMI, Body Fat and Lean Mass

Body composition describes the different components that make up body weight. Looking beyond scale weight can provide additional context, but no single measurement gives a complete picture of health.

Body weight and BMI

Body weight is the total mass of the body. BMI relates weight to height and is used as a broad screening measure. It does not directly measure body fat, muscle, bone density or fat distribution.

Body fat percentage

Body fat percentage estimates the proportion of total body weight that is fat. Methods vary in accuracy and should be interpreted as estimates.

Lean body mass

Lean body mass generally means body weight minus fat mass. It includes muscle, bones, organs and body water; it does not mean muscle alone.

Use measures in context

BMI, body-fat estimates and lean-mass estimates each answer different questions. Consider them alongside waist measurements, activity, nutrition, fitness, medical history and professional advice when appropriate.
