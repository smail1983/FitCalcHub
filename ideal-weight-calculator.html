How this calculator works

This tool displays three commonly cited historical equations: Devine, Robinson and Miller. Each uses height and a sex-specific constant. The formulas were developed for different contexts and can produce different estimates, so the results are shown side by side rather than combined into one supposedly exact number.

Important limitations

These formulas do not directly measure body fat, muscle mass, waist circumference, fitness or overall health. They do not account for every individual's body composition, age, medical history or circumstances. The outputs should not be treated as a personal goal, diagnosis or prescription.

For heights below 152.4 cm (5 ft), these equations extrapolate below their 5-foot reference point. The resulting estimates may be less reliable, so interpret them with extra caution and do not use them as a target.

For a broader view, consider health markers and personal context with a qualified healthcare professional. Avoid making extreme diet or exercise changes based only on a calculator result.

Limits and safety

This tool is designed for general adult education. It is not intended for children or teenagers, and it may not be appropriate during pregnancy or breastfeeding, for people with a history of disordered eating, or for people with medical conditions that affect nutrition or metabolism. Seek individualized advice from a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian when needed.
