BMI vs Body Fat Percentage: Which Is More Useful?

BMI and body fat percentage answer different questions. BMI relates weight to height; body fat percentage estimates how much of total body weight is fat.

What BMI can tell you

BMI is quick, inexpensive and accessible, which makes it useful for broad screening. It does not directly measure body composition and cannot distinguish muscle from fat or show where fat is stored.

What body fat percentage can add

A body-fat estimate can provide additional information about body composition, particularly when muscle mass differs between people. Measurement methods vary and have different limitations, so an estimate should not be treated as an exact measurement.

Neither measure tells the whole story

Waist circumference, blood pressure, laboratory results, fitness, lifestyle and medical history can all add useful context. Neither BMI nor body-fat percentage alone provides a complete assessment of health.
