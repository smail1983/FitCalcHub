Daily Protein Intake: How Much Do You Need?

Protein needs vary with body size, age, activity, training goals and health circumstances. There is no single target that fits everyone.

Start with the daily total

Your overall daily protein intake is a more useful starting point than trying to hit one perfect amount at every meal. A consistent pattern can make it easier to meet individual needs.

Spread protein across meals

Including a protein source at breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks can help distribute intake through the day. Options include eggs, yogurt and other dairy foods, fish, poultry, beans, lentils and tofu.

Match intake to your circumstances

People who train regularly may have different protein needs from sedentary adults. Body size, training demands, overall calorie intake and dietary pattern all matter.

Build a balanced eating pattern

Protein foods can be part of meals that also include vegetables, fruit, whole grains and other nutrient-dense choices. Do not copy another person's target without considering your own needs.

When to seek advice

For individualized nutrition advice, especially with kidney disease or another medical condition, speak with a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian.
