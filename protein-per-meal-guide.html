How Much Protein Should I Eat Per Meal?

Protein needs vary by body size, age, activity and goals. Rather than searching for one perfect serving, it is useful to think about your total daily intake and distribute protein across meals.

Total daily protein comes first

Your overall daily intake is more important than hitting an exact amount at every meal. A consistent pattern can make it easier to meet your needs.

Spread protein across the day

Including a protein source at breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks can help distribute intake. Foods such as eggs, yogurt, fish, poultry, beans, lentils and tofu can contribute protein.

Match your goal

People who train regularly may have different protein needs from sedentary adults. Your body size and training demands should be considered rather than copying another person's target.
