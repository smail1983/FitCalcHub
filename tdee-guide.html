TDEE Calculator Guide

Updated September 2026

What does TDEE mean?

TDEE stands for total daily energy expenditure. It is an estimate of the calories your body uses in a typical day, including resting energy needs and activity.

How TDEE is estimated

A common approach is to estimate basal metabolic rate (BMR) from age, sex, height and weight, then multiply BMR by an activity factor. This produces an estimate, not a direct measurement.

Why your result can change

Actual energy expenditure can vary with body composition, movement outside exercise, training volume, sleep, illness and changes in body weight. Treat a calculator result as a starting estimate and adjust based on real-world trends when appropriate.

Using TDEE for goals

People may use a TDEE estimate to plan maintenance calories or create a calorie surplus or deficit. There is no single calorie target that is appropriate for everyone. For health conditions or special circumstances, ask a qualified professional.
