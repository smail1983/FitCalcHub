How Many Calories Should I Eat a Day?

Your daily calorie needs depend on your age, sex, height, weight, and activity level. A useful starting point is your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE), which estimates how many calories you burn each day.

Start with BMR

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) estimates the energy your body uses at rest. Your activity level is then used to estimate TDEE.

What is a calorie target?

For weight maintenance, many people start around their estimated TDEE. For weight loss, a moderate calorie reduction can be used. Individual needs vary, so treat calculator results as estimates rather than medical advice.
