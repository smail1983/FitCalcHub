Body Fat Percentage vs BMI: Which Is More Useful?

BMI and body fat percentage answer different questions. BMI relates body weight to height, while body fat percentage estimates how much of total body weight is fat.

BMI is simple and accessible

BMI is easy to calculate and useful for broad screening. However, it does not directly measure body composition.

Body fat percentage adds body-composition information

A body-fat estimate can provide information that BMI cannot, especially when muscle mass differs substantially between people. However, measurement methods also have limitations and varying accuracy.

Neither measure tells the whole story

Fitness, waist circumference, blood pressure, laboratory results, lifestyle and medical history can all add useful context.

Choose the right tool for the question

Use BMI for a quick screening calculation and body-fat estimates when body composition is the focus. Avoid using either number alone to judge overall health.
