How BMI is calculated

Body mass index (BMI) is a screening estimate based on a person's weight in relation to height. For measurements in kilograms and metres, the formula is BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². For example, a weight of 70 kg and a height of 1.75 m gives a BMI of about 22.9.

Adult BMI categories

  • Below 18.5: underweight range
  • 18.5 to 24.9: healthy-weight range
  • 25.0 to 29.9: overweight range
  • 30.0 or above: obesity range

These are commonly used adult screening categories, not a diagnosis. BMI does not directly measure body fat or show where fat is distributed. It may not describe health accurately for every person, including some athletes, older adults, and people with different body compositions.

How to use your result

Enter your weight in kilograms and height in centimetres, then select Calculate. Check that both values are current and entered in the correct units. A small change in height or weight can change the result, so treat the number as an estimate rather than a precise assessment.

When BMI may not be suitable

This calculator is intended for general adult information. Adult BMI categories should not be used to assess children or teenagers; their results require age- and sex-specific growth references. BMI alone is also not a substitute for a medical assessment. If you are concerned about weight, nutrition, or a health condition, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
