BMR vs TDEE: What's the Difference?

BMR and TDEE are related but they answer different questions. BMR estimates energy used at rest, while TDEE estimates total daily energy expenditure after accounting for activity.

BMR

Basal Metabolic Rate is an estimate of the calories your body needs for basic functions at rest.

TDEE

Total Daily Energy Expenditure builds on BMR by accounting for your typical activity level. It is commonly used as a starting point for calorie planning.

Which should you use?

Use BMR when you want to understand your resting energy needs. Use TDEE when you want an estimate of your overall daily calorie expenditure.
